# TypeMaster — Modern Typing Practice Suite (Web & Desktop App)

> **The Ultimate Next-Gen Typing Platform for Speed, Accuracy, Literature, Code, and Classroom Education.**

---

## 📌 Overview

**TypeMaster** is a high-performance, distraction-free typing platform designed to help students, developers, professionals, and literature enthusiasts master touch typing, boost typing speed (WPM), and build muscle memory.

Whether you want to practice online directly in your web browser or run an offline classroom lab in a school environment with zero internet access, TypeMaster provides a unified, beautifully styled experience.

- 🌐 **Online Web Application**: Practice instantly online with full access to story mode, coding drills, standard lessons, and live typing speed tests.
- 💻 **Offline Desktop Application**: A lightweight, ultra-fast native desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux — operating 100% offline with complete data privacy, zero telemetry, and built-in School Education Portals.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Practice Modes

### 📚 1. Story Mode (Classic Literature Typing)

Practice touch typing by reading and typing actual classic literature, novels, and historical stories instead of repetitive random words.

- **Bite-Sized Sublevels**: Long books are divided into 700-word sublevels for manageable daily practice sessions.
- **Rich Literature Library**: Practice with classic works by William Faulkner, James Joyce, George MacDonald, and more.
- **Real-Time Analytics**: Monitor your WPM, accuracy percentage, elapsed time, and sublevel progression.

### 💻 2. Code Mode (Developer Syntax Drills)

Boost your code typing speed and accuracy across major programming languages:

- **Languages Supported**: **JavaScript**, **TypeScript**, **Python**, **C++**, **HTML & CSS**, and **SQL**.
- **IDE Visual Cues**: Authentic syntax highlighting, line numbers, and indentation structures.
- **Smooth Auto-Scrolling**: Proactive horizontal and vertical scrolling keeps your active line and cursor comfortably centered.

### 🎯 3. Standard Lessons & Speed Tests

- **Finger Alignment Drills**: Practice home row, top row, bottom row, numbers, and special symbols.
- **Real-Time Audio & Visual Feedback**: Optional mechanical keyboard sounds, visual key guidance, and error indicators.
- **Timed Speed Tests**: Evaluate your typing speed with 1-minute, 3-minute, and 5-minute typing tests.

### 🏫 4. School & Education Portal (Desktop App)

A complete offline education platform designed for computer labs and classroom learning:

- **Multi-Role System**: Dedicated workspaces for School Admins, Teachers, Students, and Guest typists.
- **Classroom Management**: Teachers can assign custom stories/drills, track student progress, and monitor live classroom sessions.
- **100% Offline Readiness**: Requires no internet connection or cloud dependency.

### 🎨 5. Premium Dark Aesthetics & Customization

- **Curated Theme Options**: Choose from modern dark themes including **Obsidian**, **Clay**, **Moss**, and **Gold**.
- **Customizable Experience**: Toggle sound effects, adjust cursor styles, and personalize your workspace.
