Book Curations

Typing out real books builds muscle memory for genuine punctuation, capitalization, and paragraph rhythm. Here are 5 standout classics you can start typing right now:

1. Dramatic Romance Sublevels Available

Wuthering Heights

by Emily Brontë

Ideal for practicing complex 19th-century sentence structures, em-dashes, and rich descriptive vocabulary.

👉 Start Typing Wuthering Heights
2. Adventure & Revenge Sublevels Available

The Count of Monte Cristo

by Alexandre Dumas

A high-tempo adventure tale. Fast narrative pacing helps build steady typing speed and long-form typing endurance.

👉 Start Typing The Count of Monte Cristo
3. Modernist Prose Sublevels Available

The Sun Also Rises

by Ernest Hemingway

Famous for short, direct sentences. Perfect for typists looking to establish a crisp, steady, metronomic key cadence.

👉 Start Typing The Sun Also Rises
4. Detective Mystery Sublevels Available

The Mysterious Affair at Styles

by Agatha Christie

Heavy dialogue practice featuring frequent quotation marks, character names, and shift key combinations.

👉 Start Typing The Mysterious Affair at Styles
5. Warm-Up & Whimsy Sublevels Available

Winnie-the-Pooh

by A. A. Milne

Lighthearted, clean, highly readable sentences. Great for a 10-minute warm-up session before coding or writing.

👉 Start Typing Winnie-the-Pooh
